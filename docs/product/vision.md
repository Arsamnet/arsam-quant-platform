# Product Vision

Build a commercial platform that helps evaluate and operate quantitative FX strategies under
explicit, reproducible, independently enforced risk controls. AI may enrich a decision but is never
an order authority. Success means measurable out-of-sample evidence, controlled operational risk,
and an auditable explanation for every decision—not guaranteed profit.

The initial product is one Windows trading node for development, deterministic backtesting, and
eventual broker demo validation. A later control plane may coordinate isolated customer nodes while
broker secrets remain local.
